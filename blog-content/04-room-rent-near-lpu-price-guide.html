The Hidden Charges to Ask About Before Paying

Two rooms with the same rent can cost very different amounts per month. Ask every owner these five questions:

  • ⚡ Electricity — included in rent or metered separately? Metered is normal, but get the rate confirmed.
  • 📶 WiFi — included or an extra split among tenants?
  • 🧾 Maintenance — is there a separate monthly "maintenance" fee on top of rent?
  • 💰 Security deposit — how much, and what are the exact refund conditions?
  • 📢 Broker commission — if a broker is involved, someone pays roughly half a month's rent. Direct booking avoids this entirely.

At Vats Apartment, WiFi, maintenance and security are included in the rent — the price you see on the booking page is the price you plan around.
