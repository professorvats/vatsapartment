The Agreement for Room Rent Near LPU: What to Read Before Signing

Even a one-page agreement deserves 10 minutes of reading. Check for:

  • 💰 Rent, due date and payment method — in writing, exact
  • ⚡ Electricity terms — included, or metered at what rate?
  • 🧾 What's included — WiFi, maintenance, water
  • 🛡️ Deposit terms — amount, and exactly when/how it's refunded
  • 📢 Notice period — usually one month; confirm before planning semester breaks
  • 🕐 House rules — guests, timings; whatever applies to you should be written, not verbal

Push back on vague clauses. A legit owner edits the agreement; a shady one says "don't worry" — which is your answer.

Paperwork Red Flags

  • 🚩 No agreement at all — "we work on trust here" means you have zero protection
  • 🚩 Original documents held by owner — never hand over originals, copies only
  • 🚩 Cash-only with no receipts — you'll have no record when the deposit "disappears"
  • 🚩 Random extra charges appearing that weren't in the agreement
