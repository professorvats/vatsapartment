# Blog SEO Plan — 100 Keywords → 28 Posts (LPU Student Bookings)

Target: capture the searches LPU students (and their parents) make when looking for rooms, PGs and
hostels near Lovely Professional University — and route them to `/book-now` at Vats Apartment.

- **100 keywords** organized into 12 clusters, mapped to **28 new posts** (16 posts × 4 keywords,
  12 posts × 3 keywords).
- Built on the paid-search research already in this repo (`GOOGLE-ADS-STRATEGY.md`,
  `google-ads-keywords.csv`) and deliberately **differentiated from the 3 seeded posts**:
  - `best-rooms-near-lpu-university-2026` owns *best rooms near LPU* + PG-vs-hostel overview
  - `pg-near-lpu-university-student-housing-guide-2026` owns the generic *PG near LPU* guide
  - `student-living-near-lpu-tips-first-time-renters-jalandhar` owns *first-time renter tips*
- Every post: primary keyword in title/H1/first paragraph, secondaries in subheads, 150–160 char
  excerpt (doubles as the meta description), FAQ section, CTA to `/book-now`, 2–4 internal links.
- All posts are pushed via `POST /api/blog` as **drafts**; review at `/admin/blog`, then publish.

**Priority key:** 🥇 high volume/high intent · 🥈 medium · 🥉 long-tail/low competition

## Publishing workflow

1. Post payloads live in `blog-content/*.json` (title, slug, excerpt, content, image_url, status=draft).
2. Push with `VATS_API_KEY=vats_... ./scripts/push-blog-posts.sh`.
3. Review drafts at `https://vatsapartment.com/admin/blog`, fix anything, publish.
4. After the code changes are deployed (path URLs + sitemap), submit
   `https://vatsapartment.com/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console.

## Notes on cannibalization

- Posts interlink rather than compete: each targets its own primary phrase; related posts link to
  each other with descriptive anchors (e.g. the Lawgate post links to the transport guide).
- Seed posts stay untouched; new posts link to them where relevant (PG guide, best-rooms guide).
- If Search Console later shows two posts competing for one query, merge the weaker post's content
  into the stronger and redirect (needs an alias route).
