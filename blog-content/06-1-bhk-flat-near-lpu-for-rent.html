The Hidden Costs of a Flat Near LPU for Rent

Beyond the rent, an empty flat means:

  • 🛏️ Furniture: bed, mattress, almirah, table, chair, fan — realistically ₹15,000–35,000 before you can sleep there
  • 📶 Internet installation: a new connection, installation fee, and a monthly plan
  • 🤝 Brokerage: commonly half to one month's rent if a broker found you the flat
  • 📄 Agreement & deposit: 11-month rent agreement, registration photocopies, and a deposit you might struggle to get back
  • 🧹 Everything else: geyser, gas, utensils, curtains, and fixing things yourself when the landlord doesn't

That's why many students conclude the flat dream costs 2–3 months of extra money and effort compared to a ready room.
