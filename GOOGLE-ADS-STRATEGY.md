2. **Phone Call** — `tel:+919992937447` link on homepage

**Key Insight:** Parents (not students) often make the final booking decision, especially for out-of-state students. Parents search differently:

| Who Searches | Keywords | Concern | Ad Messaging |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Student (18-22)** | "cheap PG near LPU", "room under 10K" | Price, WiFi, friends nearby | Price + Amenities + Social |
| **Parent (40-55)** | "safe accommodation near LPU", "LPU student housing" | Safety, security, food, distance | Security + Proximity + Reviews |
| **Student (23-28)** | "furnished flat near LPU", "1 BHK near LPU" | Privacy, quality, independence | Privacy + Features + Kitchen |
| **International** | "LPU accommodation international students" | Trust, advance booking, payment | Website quality + Reviews + WhatsApp |

**Action:** Create separate ad groups for Parent-focused vs Student-focused messaging.

## 5.5 Why NOT to Target "All India" with One Campaign

**DON'T:** Create one campaign targeting all of India with ₹400/day budget.

**Why:**
- Delhi/Mumbai CPC is ₹15-25 — your entire budget gets spent in 20 clicks
- Bihar/UP CPC is ₹3-8 — you get 5x more clicks there
- Punjab local CPC is ₹2-5 — you get maximum clicks here

**DO:** Create separate campaigns per tier with different budgets. Let Google optimize within each tier.
