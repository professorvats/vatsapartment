Finding a Room Near LPU University: Start With Location

Almost every LPU student starts with the same search: a room near LPU University, close enough to walk to class, cheap enough to survive the month. The good news is that the campus is surrounded by student housing on every side. The catch is that "near LPU" can mean anything from a 5-minute walk to a 30-minute auto ride — and the rent and your daily routine change completely depending on which pocket you pick.

This guide breaks down the areas around Lovely Professional University, what rooms near LPU actually cost in each, and how to choose the right one before you pay any advance.

What Your Rent Should Include

When you compare rooms near LPU, don't compare rent alone — compare what comes inside the rent:

  • ✅ Furniture — bed, almirah, study table and chair. Furnishing an empty room yourself costs ₹15,000–25,000 upfront.
  • ✅ Internet — high-speed WiFi is essential for LPU's online portal, classes and assignments.
  • ✅ Private bathroom and kitchen — the difference between a PG and a hostel experience.
  • ✅ Security — CCTV coverage and a secure building entry.
  • ✅ Maintenance — someone actually fixes things when they break.

How to Choose Before You Visit

1. Put distance first

Ask for the exact landmark and walk the route on Google Maps. "Near LPU" listings sometimes turn out to be two villages away.

2. Ask what the rent includes

WiFi, water, electricity, maintenance — get the full list on WhatsApp before you travel. A ₹7,000 room that bills electricity separately can end up costlier than a ₹9,000 all-inclusive one.

3. Book before the rush

The best rooms near campus are gone by July. If you're joining in the new session, book your accommodation during admission season, not after orientation.
