How to Test Any PG Near LPU With WiFi (5 Minutes)

Don't accept "WiFi hai" as an answer. During the visit or video call:

  1. Connect your own phone/laptop to the room's network — not the owner's hotspot.
  2. Run a speed test twice — once normally, once with a video call running. Upload speed matters as much as download for classes.
  3. Ask who shares the connection — one fiber line for a 30-room building at peak hour is very different from one line for 6 rooms.
  4. Ask the router's location — if it's two floors down, your room's signal is a lottery.
  5. Ask the backup plan — what happens when the line goes down for a day during submissions?
