Where the Difference Between LPU Hostel and Private PG Really Shows

The hostel fee looks moderate until you add mess charges and notice that sharing still means 2–4 people per room. Rules

If campus events, late library hours or project deadlines are part of your LPU plan, hostel curfews and entry registers become a real constraint. No-curfew independent rooms trade some supervision for serious flexibility — a big reason seniors eventually move out.

A shared hostel room means your sleep and study schedule are set by committee. A private room means 2 AM study sessions or 8 AM classes on your terms, with your own study table and reliable WiFi for the LMS.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is PG cheaper than LPU hostel?

A: Usually yes when shared: ₹4,500/person for a furnished room with private bathroom vs ₹8,000–12,000 plus mess charges in hostel. Even alone at ₹9,000, you're in the same range with far more comfort.

Q: Do LPU students prefer hostel or PG?

A: First-years often take the hostel for the campus experience; from second year onward many switch to private furnished rooms for privacy, food and freedom.

Q: Can I leave the hostel and move to a PG mid-session?

A: Yes — students do it every semester. Check the hostel's refund policy first, then book the room before giving up the hostel seat. Vats takes direct bookings year-round, subject to availability.
