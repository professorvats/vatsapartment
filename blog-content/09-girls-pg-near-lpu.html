The 8-Point Safety Checklist

Before booking any ladies PG near LPU — or any accommodation as a female student — verify all of these:

  1. 📹 CCTV in common areas — entrances, stairs, parking. Ask to see the actual cameras, not a poster.
  2. 🚪 Secure building entry — a main gate that's actually locked at night, not an open staircase.
  3. 👤 Reachable owner/warden — call the number in the listing. If nobody answers during booking, they won't answer during an emergency either.
  4. 📍 Location and lighting — the walk from the main road to the building should be lit and busy, not a dark empty stretch.
  5. 👩‍🤝‍👩 Who lives in the building — all-students, mixed, or families? There's no single right answer, but you should know before moving in.
  6. 🕐 Written entry rules — whatever the timing policy is, get it in the agreement, in both directions (your obligations and the owner's).
  7. 🚿 Private bathroom — non-negotiable for most female students; shared-floor bathrooms are the biggest day-to-day complaint in cheap PGs.
  8. 🤝 A parent video call — any legit owner will happily give mom a live tour. We insist on it for remote bookings.
