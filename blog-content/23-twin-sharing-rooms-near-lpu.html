The 5 Rules Every Sharing Pair Should Agree On (Day One)

  1. Rent and bills: who pays the owner, by which date, and how the other reimburses. In writing, even between best friends.
  2. Stuff ownership: what's shared (fridge, TV) vs personal. Labeled shelves solve 90% of kitchen wars.
  3. Quiet hours during exams: agree the fortnight before exams both adjust — this rule saves friendships.
  4. Guests: a heads-up norm, not a permission system.
  5. Exit plan: if one wants to move out mid-session, notice period and how the room gets re-shared. Decide before it's awkward.
