Food: Mess, Tiffin or Cooking?

After rent, food is the biggest line — and where the biggest savings hide:

  • 🍛 Mess/hostel food: fixed monthly charges whether you eat every meal or not; the menu is the menu.
  • 🍱 Tiffin services near campus: typically ₹2,500–3,500/month, decent variety, zero effort.
  • 🍳 Cooking with a private kitchen: groceries for one often land at ₹2,000–2,500/month — and a private kitchen (fridge included) means you actually can. Mix with the dhabas around Apna Chai Wala for the nights you can't cook.
