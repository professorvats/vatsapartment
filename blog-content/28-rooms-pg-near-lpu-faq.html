Distances & Locations

Q: How far is Lawgate from LPU?

A: Lawgate is the student market stretch along the campus side — most PGs and rooms there are a 5–15 minute walk or short auto ride from the academic blocks. Vats Apartment sits on the main-gate side near Apna Chai Wala, about 10 minutes' walk from campus.

Q: Which area should I live in?

A: If you're on campus most days: the main-gate/Apna Chai Wala pocket or Lawgate — both walkable. Only consider Phagwara town if you're on campus 3–4 days a week. The full comparison is in where to stay near LPU.

Rents & Money

Q: What does a room near LPU cost per month?

A: Shared rooms ₹4,000–6,000; private rooms with common bathrooms ₹6,000–8,000; fully furnished rooms with private bathrooms ₹9,000–14,000. Vats standard rooms are ₹9,000–10,000 (₹4,500 each when shared), rooftop deluxe ₹14,000. Full breakdown: price guide.

Q: Do owners take a security deposit?

A: Practices vary; many ask for about a month's rent. Vats holds your room with a ₹2,000 booking amount instead — get whatever deposit terms exist in writing.

Q: Is brokerage normal?

A: With brokers, yes — half to one month's rent. Direct owner bookings (like Vats) charge nothing. Details: no-brokerage guide.

Food, Rules & Daily Life

Q: Do PGs near LPU include food?

A: Mess-food PGs charge extra (typically ₹3,000–4,500/month) on a fixed menu. Furnished rooms with private kitchens — like every Vats unit — let you cook (₹2,000–2,500/month in groceries) or use the dhabas and tiffin services around campus. Most tenants find that cheaper and better. Full math: cost of living guide.

Q: Do PGs near LPU have curfews?

A: Traditional PGs often lock gates at 9–10 PM. Vats Apartment has no curfew — your room, your key, your schedule.

Q: Are guests allowed?

A: Depends on the building's rules — ask and get it in the agreement. Common areas at Vats handle meetups fine; just keep the owner informed as a courtesy.

Q: Is WiFi included?

A: At good buildings, yes. Vats includes building-wide fiber WiFi in the rent. At cheap PGs, verify with a live speed test — "WiFi hai" is not data. More: WiFi & study guide.
